Before you book anywhere

Ask these three things first

1. Ask what the rent includes

Food, power, wifi — ask for each one in writing before you sign anything. Vague answers turn into arguments later.

2. Visit unannounced once

Look at the place at 8pm on a weekday, not just in the morning. Ours looks the same as the photos.

3. Ask who already lives there

Shifts, how long they've stayed, whether the kitchen actually runs every day. A house is its people.

Then look at ours. The photos are the actual rooms, and we'll answer anything you ask.
