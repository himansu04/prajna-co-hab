About us

Run by the family that owns it.

One property, owned and run by one family.

What Prajna means to us

The name comes from the family. Ten beds across four twin-sharing and two single rooms, and the full ground floor for living — kitchen, dining hall, TV lounge, reception.

How it is run

Rent conversations happen directly with the family. No commission in your move-in, no surprise charges. Problems get fixed by the people who own the place.

Who it is for

Working professionals in the Bhosari–Chakan belt — shift workers, people new to Pune, people saving. Live minutes from work, eat home food, keep your money.

The promise

  • Clean rooms and clean common spaces, maintained daily
  • Straight pricing
  • Safety first: CCTV entry, owner contact available
  • Real photos, real answers, visits before any payment
