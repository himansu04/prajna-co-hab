About us

A home run by the family that owns it.

Prajna Co-hab is not a chain, not an agent's inventory, and not a listing managed by some far-away operator. It is one property, owned and run by one family — and that is the whole point.

What Prajna means to us

The name comes from the family, and the idea behind it is simple: a clear-headed, well-kept place to live while you build your working life. The house was planned as a co-living home from the start — ten beds across four twin-sharing and two single rooms, with a full ground floor given to living: kitchen, dining hall, a proper TV lounge, and a real entrance and reception.

How it is run

Owner-managed, every day. Rent conversations happen directly with the family — no broker in the middle, no commission buried in your move-in, no surprise "charges" on the bill. Problems get fixed by the people who own the place, because the people who own the place are the people you talk to.

Who it is for

Working professionals in the Bhosari–Chakan industrial belt — people on shifts, people new to Pune, people saving for something bigger. If your day starts at a factory gate nearby, this was built for your routine: live minutes from work, eat home-style food, keep your money.

The promise

  • Clean rooms and clean common spaces, maintained daily
  • Straight pricing — what we quote is what you pay
  • Safety first: CCTV entry, owner contact available
  • Real photos, real answers, visits before any payment
