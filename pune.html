City guide

New to Pune?

For people moving for work, not tourists. What the city runs on, what it costs, how the commute works.

Why people come

Pune runs on three engines: the MIDC factory belt (Bhosari, Chakan, Talegaon), IT parks (Hinjawadi), and education. The Pimpri-Chinchwad side — where the PG sits — is the factory belt: steady shift work, lower living costs, everything within a short ride.

Getting around

Two-wheelers rule this city — cheap to run, fastest door to gate. PMPML buses cover the PCMC side, the metro is expanding here, autos fill the gaps.

Weather, month by month

March–May runs hot. June–September is monsoon; the hills turn green. October–February is dry and pleasant.

Cost of living

The PCMC and MIDC belt is the budget side of Pune. A PG here keeps your two biggest costs — rent and commute — small.

Food

Maharashtrian staples everywhere: misal, vada pav, poha, cheap thalis. The belt is packed with mess-style eateries built for shift workers.

Culture

Ganesh Chaturthi is a whole-city festival; Diwali lights up every lane. Temples, forts and treks within weekend distance. Friendlier pace than Mumbai.
