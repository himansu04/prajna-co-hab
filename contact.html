Straight answers

Questions people ask

Short answers, because they’re the useful ones. Anything else, write to us.

Is there a notice period?

Three months to start with, then one month's notice to leave. Both are on one page you sign before you move in.

Can friends or family visit?

Yes, in the common areas during the day. If someone is staying over, tell us first so the caretaker knows.

How does the deposit come back?

Rs 12,000 for double sharing, Rs 14,000 for a private room. It comes back when you move out, after the place is checked over. Both figures are on the page you sign.

Is food included?

It depends on the plan you pick. The "with dinner" rate includes dinner only. Breakfast and lunch are not included at either rate — you'd buy those yourself. The kitchen is fully fitted and shared either way.

What about parking?

Covered two-wheeler parking, included. Four-wheelers — email us and we'll tell you what's free at the time.

Electricity — who pays?

Metered and shared across the house. It is the one bill not included in the rent. Water, housekeeping and the caretaker are included.

I work odd shift hours. Lockout time?

There's a caretaker round the clock. Give your shift timings once and they'll keep the gate open for you.

How do I actually book a bed?

Email us, come and look at the room, sign one page, and you're in. Nothing is charged until you've seen the place. There's a three-month lock-in, then one month's notice.
