Straight answers

Common questions.

Short answers. Anything else, WhatsApp us. sample — owner confirms final wording

Is there a notice period?

One month, as per the agreement.

Can friends or family visit?

Yes, in common areas during the day. Overnight guests: inform the owner.

How does the deposit come back?

Refundable per the agreement, settled at move-out.

Is food included for single rooms too?

Same kitchen, same answer. See the room cards on the home page.

What about parking?

Covered two-wheeler parking, included. Four-wheelers: ask.

Electricity — who pays?

Metered and split per room.

I work odd shift hours. Lockout time?

No lockout for shift hours. Tell the owner your timing once.

How do I actually book a bed?

WhatsApp the owner, visit, sign one page, move in.
